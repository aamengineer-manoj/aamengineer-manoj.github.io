06 / tips

Six tips from 500+ interviews.

01

Think aloud

Silence reads as being stuck. Say your approach, the trade-offs and why you picked one, even before you write code.

02

Know your own project

Expect follow-ups on every line of your resume. If you can't explain why a design choice was made, drop it from the page.

03

Clarify before you solve

Ask about input size, edge cases and constraints first. It shows judgment, and it saves you from solving the wrong problem.

04

Start simple, then improve

Give the brute-force answer, state its cost, then optimise. A working answer you can reason about beats a clever one you can't.

05

Practise out loud

Reading solutions is not the same as explaining them. Do mocks, record yourself and fix the places where you ramble.

06

Treat rejections as data

After each round, write down what was weak and fix that one thing. Twenty rejections taught me more than any course.
